Our story

The time you need, not the time you’re given.

Ray-Lewis Opticians was three decades in the making. Owner and optometrist Pramod-Ray Patel started out with the well-known opticians Dollond & Aitchison, then built his career across the NHS, the private sector and his own practices in South Yorkshire and North Derbyshire.

Along the way, one thing kept bothering him: patients were too often seen for the slot they had been booked into, rather than for as long as they actually needed. That lack of personal care became the reason Ray-Lewis exists.

Today every patient's eyesight and lifestyle are looked at in full. It's a different way of thinking about patient care, built around getting to know you and protecting your vision for years to come.

Knowledge centre

Questions, answered.

Straightforward answers to the things our patients ask us most. Can't see yours? Just ask us in practice.

Why should I choose Ray-Lewis Opticians?
We’re a truly independent practice. Eye tests are never rushed, styling advice is free, and we use advanced equipment like our Triton 3D scanner to look after your eyes over the long term.
How often should I get my eyes tested?
The NHS advises most adults to have an eye test every two years. Your optometrist may suggest more frequent checks, for example if you have diabetes, a family history of glaucoma, or are over 70. Children are often seen every year.
My vision feels fine. Do I still need an eye examination?
Yes. Some eye conditions, such as glaucoma, develop without noticeable symptoms at first. A thorough eye examination can pick up early changes, and can also flag signs of wider health issues like diabetes and high blood pressure.
Should I book my child in for an eye examination?
Definitely. Children don't always realise they can't see clearly, and good vision matters for learning. NHS eye tests are free for under-16s, and for under-19s in full-time education.
Do you have an OCT to take 3D scans of my eyes?
We do. Our Triton 3D scanner captures detailed images of the layers at the back of your eye, so we can track your eye health over time and talk you through the results on screen.
I struggle to find glasses that suit me. Can you help?
That's exactly what our free styling consultation is for. We'll look at your face shape, colouring and lifestyle, then help you try on frames until you find the pair that feels right.
Do you stock designer frames?
Yes, alongside independent and handmade frames. Our collection changes regularly, so pop in to see what's new.
